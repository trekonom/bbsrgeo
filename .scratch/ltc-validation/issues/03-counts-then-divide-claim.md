**Category:** bug (documentation claim)
**Summary:** The vignette's "Converting the underlying counts instead" section (`vignettes/bbsrgeo.Rmd`) calls counts-then-divide "the more broadly robust technique" and says the crosswalk's `population_old` and `ltcregio$population_total` are "nearly the same series". The first is not supported by the data; the second holds only partly.

**Verified (all 400 current districts, all 8 years, vs `ltcinkar`):**
- Ratio and counts-then-divide differ by more than 0.005 in only 1% of districts in 2009, 0.5% in 2011 and 2013, and none from 2015 on. Median error vs INKAR is the same for both in every year.
- Where they differ, neither is consistently closer to INKAR (counts is no better for Rhein-Hunsrueck-Kreis 07140000, closer for Goettingen 03159000 and two Mecklenburg-Vorpommern districts).
- Crosswalk `population_old` * 1000 / `ltcregio$population_total` has median ~1.000 for year_from 2009 and 2013-2021. Exceptions: year_from 2011 has 14 districts off by 5-8% (and 07235000 has `population_old = 1` vs 143,552, a likely source error); year_from 2023 has `population_old` in persons, not thousands, for the whole sheet.

**Required changes (`vignettes/bbsrgeo.Rmd`, closing paragraphs of "Converting the underlying counts instead"):**

Preferring counts when numerator and denominator are available is a consistency argument, not a demonstrated accuracy gain; say so.
2. Restrict the "nearly the same series" statement to what was checked (2009 and 2013-2021, apart from a few 2011 districts), or remove it.
3. Keep `type = "ratio"` as the option when only the ratio is available (that sentence is fine).
