# Report shares that disagree with the counts, without changing the conversion

Status: ready-for-agent
Category: enhancement

Split off from issue 01 (decision: keep the shares, ADR 0001). Wrong shares
that sum to exactly 1 are neither corrected nor detected today.

## Observation

For multi-row groups whose counts differ between rows, `count / sum(count)`
agrees with the share within 0.011 in almost all cases (area: 244 of 248
groups; population outside 1990-2000: 136 of 136). The disagreements are the
four 1990-2000 share-of-1 groups (already handled by the off-1 rule) and the
1990-2000 population groups, mostly explained by counts rounded to one decimal
(issue 01 triage comment). So a disagreement check would today flag only known
cases; its value is catching new ones in future BBSR files.

## To establish

1. Where it reports: a pass in `bbsr_crosswalk_read()` next to the share-sum
   warning, a helper for users, or a test on the cached files only.
2. How to avoid reporting the rounded 1990-2000 population counts (a tolerance
   from the count rounding, as in `SHARE_SUM_TOLERANCE`, stated with its
   measured basis).
3. Whether groups with equal counts are skipped (they cannot be told from the
   unit's total repeated on each row; ADR 0001).

## Out of scope

Changing how `harmonize_regions()` converts (ADR 0001). Correcting the BBSR
data.

- Item 2 (rounded population counts): in the 1990-2000 file 145 rows of 70 groups
  have counts between 0.1 and 38.3, 84 % of them multiples of 0.1. A bound of
  `0.05 * (1 + n_rows) / total` per row (each count off by at most 0.05) is
  exceeded by none of the 145 rows; the largest deviation reaches 27 % of that
  bound. So the 1990-2000 deviations (up to 0.051) are rounding, and the bound
  is derived from the counts' resolution, not fitted to the shares.
- Districts: one group exceeds the same bound by far: 07235000, 2011, whose
  shares sum to 1.009 (area) and 1.007 (population). Cause (maintainer, checked
  against the district sheets 2010-2011 and 2011-2012): a miscoded `ags_old`.
  The 2011 -> 2012 sheet has a row with `name_old` Bernkastel-Wittlich but
  `ags_old` 07235000 (Trier-Saarburg), going to 07235000, with `area_old` 10.1
  and `share_area` 0.00858. Bernkastel-Wittlich is 07231000: its own row in that
  sheet sums to 0.991 (`area_old` 1168 against 1178 in 2010 -> 2011, a loss of
  10.1 km2), and 10.1 / 1178 = 0.00858. So the row belongs to 07231000, and the
  sum of 0.991 for 07231000 and 1.009 for 07235000 both come from it. With the
  default renormalization (commit 46ad0a2) both groups are rescaled to 1:
  Trier-Saarburg keeps its total (its own row and the miscoded row both map to
  itself), but Bernkastel-Wittlich keeps all of its value instead of passing
  0.86 % on to Trier-Saarburg.
  The municipal 2000-2010 population deviation of 0.0072 was not compared with
  the bound.
- Item 3 (equal counts): skipped. They cannot be told from the unit's total
  repeated on every row (ADR 0001), and the targets' own areas do not resolve
  them (issue 01 triage comment).

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** `bbsr_crosswalk_read()` reports, in one message, the `ags_old`/transition groups whose shares disagree with their own counts by more than the counts' resolution allows. No change to any converted number.

**Current behavior:**
`bbsrgeo_check_share_sums()` only looks at share sums. A group whose shares sum to 1 but do not match `count / total` (07235000, 2011, in the district file) is not reported.

**Desired behavior:**
- For each weight basis (`area`, `population`, `employment`) with a `<basis>_old` column: for groups with more than one target row, counts all observed and non-negative, differing between rows (as in `renormalize_forward_shares()`, `COUNT_DIFFERENCE_TOLERANCE`) and a positive total, compare `count / total` with the basis's share per row.
- A row disagrees when the deviation exceeds `COUNT_RESOLUTION * (1 + n_rows) / total`, with a new named constant `COUNT_RESOLUTION <- 0.05` (half of the coarsest observed rounding, 0.1; state this in the comment).
- Groups with equal counts are skipped, and groups whose shares sum off 1 by more than `SHARE_SUM_TOLERANCE` are skipped (the off-1 rule handles them).
- One `rlang::inform()` per read, naming the number of groups, the basis and up to five examples (`ags_old`, `year_from`, the largest deviation), and saying that the conversion uses the shares as given. It states no cause.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Tests on small constructed tibbles: agreeing group (no message), disagreeing group (message with example), deviation within the resolution bound (no message), equal counts (skipped), off-1 group (skipped), missing `*_old` column (no error).
- [ ] On the five cached files the message is measured and the result recorded in the real-file test in `test-crosswalk-read.R`. Expected from triage: only 07235000 (2011) in the district file. If other groups appear (the municipal 2000-2010 population deviation of 0.0072 is not checked against the bound), investigate them before changing `COUNT_RESOLUTION`; do not loosen the bound to make them pass.
- [ ] No change to `harmonize_regions()` output; all existing tests pass.
- [ ] `GLOSSARY.md` gets a short entry for the check if it introduces a term.

**Out of scope:**
- Changing how shares are used (ADR 0001) or correcting the BBSR data.
- Recoding the miscoded 07235000 (2011 -> 2012) row; see the triage comment. A
  check of `name_old` against `ags_old` is a separate decision.
